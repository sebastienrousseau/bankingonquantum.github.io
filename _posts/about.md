# About Banking On Quantum

Banking On Quantum is an international research and advisory initiative dedicated to preparing global financial infrastructure for the quantum era.

## Strategic Objectives

1. **Protect Global Value**: Eliminate vulnerability to Shor's algorithm by accelerating post-quantum cryptographic transitions across banks, payment networks, and sovereign wealth funds.
2. **Democratize Quantum Finance**: Provide platform-agnostic algorithmic toolkits that enable financial analysts to execute quantum algorithms on diverse QPU hardware.
3. **Regulatory Guidance**: Collaborate with central banks, the BIS Innovation Hub, and international standard-setting bodies to establish pragmatic quantum security mandates.
