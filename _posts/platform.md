# Quantum Platform Architecture

Banking On Quantum establishes a vendor-agnostic, cryptographically agile framework that integrates hybrid classical-quantum computing into existing banking cores.

## Core Architectural Layers

### 1. Cryptographic Agility & Key Management
- **NIST Post-Quantum Standards**: Native support for ML-KEM (Kyber) for key encapsulation, ML-DSA (Dilithium) for digital signatures, and SLH-DSA (SPHINCS+) for stateless hash-based signing.
- **Hybrid TLS & Dual-Certificates**: Concurrent validation of classical RSA/ECDSA and post-quantum keys ensuring zero downtime during multi-year institutional migration.

### 2. Quantum Algorithm Acceleration Engine
- **Quantum Amplitude Estimation (QAE)**: Quadratic speedups for Value-at-Risk (VaR) and Expected Shortfall (ES) calculations.
- **QAOA & VQE Solvers**: Optimal collateral allocation, cash-sweep optimization, and trade-clearing batch schedules.

### 3. Hardware-Agnostic Abstraction Layer
- **QPU Interoperability**: Seamless execution across superconducting qubits, trapped-ion processors, photonic quantum hardware, and classical quantum simulators.
