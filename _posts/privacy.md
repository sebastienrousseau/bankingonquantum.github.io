# Privacy Policy

We uphold the highest standards of data confidentiality and privacy protection.

## Data Governance
- **No Third-Party Analytics**: We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking scripts.
- **Communication Security**: Contact inquiries are processed securely and used exclusively to respond to specific research or advisory requests.
- **Zero Data Monetization**: We never sell or license visitor data to external parties.
